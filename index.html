A note from the founder

Hey Designers,

Thanks for reading this far, and for however far you've come as a designer. Design isn't easy. It's hard work, trial and error, and feelings most of us carry alone.

For me, my design career has left me frustrated more often than not, craving things I didn't have: people who related to me, real conversations, and a personal outlet for it all.

I loved sharing, but most people were too busy or just not interested. Even when I did get the chance to chat, I didn't want them to think less of me. So I kept it to myself, which turned into constant overthinking, many slow days, and a lot of self-doubt. I often wondered if I was even cut out for this.

Sound familiar? That's exactly why I built Design Diaries. It's a place where you can be honest about where you're at, reflect on your progress, and see what others are working through, all without being judged.

Every entry comes from a designer like you. I hope you'll add yours too.

Keep sharing,
Dason

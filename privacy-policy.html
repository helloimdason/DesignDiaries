Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Design Diaries ("we," "us," "our") is an anonymous space for early-career and student designers to share reflections, advice, wins, and struggles. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. We use Google Sign-In to authenticate you. We receive basic profile information from Google (your email address and account ID) to create and manage your account. We do not display your name, email, or Google profile photo anywhere on Design Diaries, your entries and activity are anonymous to other users.

Content you create. This includes diary entries, prompts you've answered, tags and topics you've selected, notebooks you've created, and reactions you've given to other entries.

Usage information. We may collect information about how you use the app, such as pages visited, features used, and general analytics data (see Section 5).

Device and technical information. We may automatically collect standard technical information such as browser type, device type, and IP address for security and troubleshooting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Let you post, edit, and delete diary entries
  • Show your entries anonymously to other users on the public feed
  • Let you organize saved entries into personal notebooks
  • Enforce posting limits and content guidelines (see our Terms of Service)
  • Investigate reports of content that violates our guidelines
  • Improve the product and understand how it's used
  • Communicate with you about your account, if necessary

3. Anonymity — What This Means in Practice

Design Diaries is built around anonymity. Here's specifically what that means:

  • Your name, email, and any other identifying information from your Google account are never shown to other users.
  • Entries you post to the public feed are visible to other users, but not attributed to you by name.
  • We can still associate entries with your account internally (for example, so you can edit or delete your own entries, or so we can investigate a report), but this connection is not exposed to other users.
  • If you report another user's entry, or another user reports yours, we may need to review account-level information as part of that investigation, but this is handled internally, not shared with the other party.

4. How Long We Keep Your Content

  • Entries you post are visible on the public feed for a limited window (currently 7 days), after which they move into your own private Diary and are no longer visible to other users.
  • You can delete any entry, at any time, permanently. Once deleted, it cannot be recovered.
  • If you delete your account, we will delete your associated content in accordance with applicable law, except where we're required to retain certain records (e.g., for legal or safety investigations already in progress).

5. Analytics and Cookies

We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate usage patterns, like which pages are visited and how features are used. This data is generally anonymized or pseudonymized and is not used to identify you individually to other users.

We use essential cookies/local storage required for authentication and basic app functionality (for example, remembering your sound settings). Update this section based on your actual analytics/cookie setup once implemented.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app (e.g., our hosting and database provider, authentication provider)
  • If required by law, or to protect the safety of our users
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can edit or delete any of your entries at any time.
  • You can save or remove entries from your notebooks at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us at info@dasonluo.com. We'll process your request and confirm once it's complete.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy law, including Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), or foreign laws such as the GDPR or CCPA if they apply to you based on your location. These rights may include the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Design Diaries is intended for users who are at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children under this age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes, we'll make reasonable efforts to notify users.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at info@dasonluo.com.
